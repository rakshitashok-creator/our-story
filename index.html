11 September 2021

I am so glad that we decided to meet that day. I had so much fun chatting about random stuff.

I first sat across from you because I thought that’s what people do on first dates, but it made me happy when you asked me to sit next to you so we could hear each other better.

You told me about yourself, and you were also so curious about me. I’ve never felt like anyone has ever wanted to know me more than you did. Even though it was the first time we met, you were so nice to me and kept asking me questions.

I remember going to the washroom and thinking about how sweet you were as a person.

Googling first-date questions to ask each other was an amazing idea too. I’m still not sure whose idea it was.

We spoke soooo much that we ended up staying at the bar until, after some tequila shots, we were finally asked to leave. 😂

It was such an amazing day, and thinking about it takes me back to a time before our relationship had even begun.

Thank you for coming out with me and showing me such a good time. You were amazing.

I wish I could relive that day with you. ❤️
